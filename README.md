# FitPlanner

FitPlanner is a modern, AI-powered fitness companion designed to help users seamlessly plan their workouts, track their progress, and receive customized dietary advice. By leveraging profile metrics and personalized goals, FitPlanner acts as a pocket personal trainer to streamline your fitness journey.

## ✨ Features

* **🔒 Secure Authentication:** Simple and secure user registration and login experience.
* **👤 Detailed User Profiles:** Track essential health indicators such as Age, Height, Weight, and automatically calculated **BMI Metrics**.
* **🤖 AI Coach Customization:**
    * **Workout Generation:** Dynamically creates a weekly training schedule customized to your fitness level (Beginner, Intermediate, Advanced) and frequency.
    * **Diet Suggestions:** Generates targeted daily calorie/protein goals alongside split-meal ideas based on your targets (e.g., *Gain Muscle*).
* **🏋️ Custom Workout Creator:** Build your own workout splits by selecting from a modular library of exercises (e.g., Bench Press, Deadlift, Push-ups).
* **📊 Performance Tracking:** Keep a close eye on your active training streaks, weekly metrics, and volume tracking over time.

* **Styling:** Custom dark-themed UI with clean layouts
